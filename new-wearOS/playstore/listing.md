# Google Play listing for 2.0

Assets and text for the store listing, checked against Google's Wear OS quality rules
(WO-G2, WO-G4, WO-G6, WO-G9). The screenshots come from
`watchface/tools/store_screenshots.sh`.

## Icon (WO-G4)

`icon.png`: 512×512, 32-bit PNG. The face in its default settings, as a circle touching
the edges, on dark gray so that the round face stands out. `icon-black.png` is the same on
black. Play rounds the corners itself.

## Feature graphic

`feature-graphic.png`: 1024×500, 24-bit PNG without transparency, as Play requires. The
name in Limelight with the face's slant and the line "Display fonts · color themes",
next to the face in two settings, made from the screenshots. Like the description, it
names no number of fonts or themes, so it stays true when fonts or themes are added.

## Screenshots (WO-G6)

`screenshots/`: square, 454×454, without a device frame, at 10:08. Six combinations of
font, color theme and 12/24 hours, then ambient mode in color and in off-white.

## Title

Slanted!

## Short description (at most 80 characters)
Big slanted digits with a selection of display fonts and color schemes.

## Full description
<b>Minimalistic watch face for Wear OS: digital watches can be beautiful!</b>
Slanted! is a digital watch face built around big, tilted numbers. The hours fill the
left half of the screen, the minutes and seconds follow along the same slant, and the date
and two complications sit beneath them.

• Several display fonts
• Multiple color themes
• 24-hour or 12-hour time
• Two complications, such as battery, steps, weather or your next event
• Always-on mode in color or in a calm off-white, kept dim to save battery

To customize it, touch and hold the watch face, then tap Edit.

Version 2.0 is rebuilt in Google's Watch Face Format and needs Wear OS 4 or later. It no
longer needs a phone app. Compared with version 1:
• the face tilts one way only, made for the left wrist;
• colors come as themes instead of one color per element;
• the date always uses the larger size;
• there are two complications instead of three.

Source code on GitHub: <a href="https://github.com/mu-lambda/slantedWatchface">https://github.com/mu-lambda/slantedWatchface</a>
